About Us
The Chamber
EuroCham Jordan is the official organization representing European companies operating in Jordan.
Learn More About Us
Our Mission
To promote and strengthen sustainable trade and investment linkages between Jordanian businesses and their European counterparts, with a focus on small and medium-sized enterprises across key economic sectors.
Our Vision
To be the leading platform for EU-Jordan economic partnership, fostering a thriving business environment that benefits members, contributes to Jordan's economic development, and strengthens Jordan–EU relations.
Our Values
Integrity, transparency, excellence, and a deep commitment to serving our members and advancing EU-Jordan economic cooperation.
