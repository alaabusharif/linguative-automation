About The Conference
Jordan–EU Investment Conference 2026
Under the Royal Patronage of His Majesty King Abdullah II and attended by Her Excellency Ursula Von der Leyen, President of the European Commission, the Jordan-EU Investment Conference 2026 is your entry point to a high-growth regional hub.
Marking the operational launch of the Strategic and Comprehensive Partnership, this event mobilizes the EU's EUR 3 billion support package to convert political momentum into executable deals. It connects European capital with a curated pipeline of bankable opportunities supported by EU de-risking instruments aligned with EU Global Gateway priorities, spanning green energy, water, digital infrastructure, and critical minerals.
Strategic Investment Windows
High-Value Industries
Jordan is advancing innovation-driven manufacturing in line with the Economic Modernization Vision. Key sectors include pharmaceuticals, chemicals, food processing, electronics, and textiles, supported by strong export networks and skilled talent. These industries position Jordan as a competitive destination for high-value industrial investment.
Regional Gateways
Jordan serves as a strategic gateway for regional trade, investment, and reconstruction across neighboring markets. With reconstruction opportunities emerging in Syria, Gaza, and Iraq, the country offers companies a stable base for regional operations. Extensive trade agreements with global partners further enhance access to international markets.
Energy
Jordan is transforming its energy sector to strengthen energy security and expand renewable energy generation. The country has emerged as a regional leader in solar and wind energy, supported by regulatory reforms and strong private sector participation. New investment opportunities are emerging in grid infrastructure, energy storage, green hydrogen, and natural gas development.
Water
Water security is a national priority for Jordan and a key area for technological innovation and infrastructure investment. Major initiatives are underway to expand desalination, improve water efficiency, and strengthen wastewater treatment and reuse. These efforts create opportunities for investment in desalination technologies, smart water management systems, and resilient water infrastructure.
Transport and Logistics
Jordan’s strategic location positions the country as a key transport and logistics hub connecting markets across Asia, Europe, and Africa. The Kingdom continues to invest in modern logistics infrastructure, including highways, ports, aviation facilities, and rail development. These initiatives strengthen Jordan’s role in regional trade and supply chain connectivity.
Digital Economy & Technology
Jordan is emerging as a regional hub for digital innovation, supported by a highly skilled workforce and a dynamic ICT ecosystem with more than 3,000 companies. With strong STEM talent and over 45,000 professionals in the sector, the country offers a competitive base for technology operations, software development, and digital services. Investment opportunities are expanding in artificial intelligence, semiconductors, and next-generation data centers.
