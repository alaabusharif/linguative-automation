Frequently Asked Questions
What is Gentex Training Center?
Gentex Training Center is a U.S.-based learning and development provider delivering professional training programs for executives, managers, engineers, and specialists across a wide range of industries.
What types of professional training courses does Gentex Training Center offer?
We offer 600+ professional training courses spanning management, leadership, engineering, finance, technology, and other specialized fields designed for professionals at every career level.
Do you offer customized/in-house corporate training?
Yes. In addition to our international training courses and public course schedule, we design customized in-house training programs tailored to your organization's goals, industry, and team needs.
Why should I choose Gentex Training Center?
With over 5 years of experience and a team built on deep industry expertise, Gentex Training Center has trained 1,000+ professionals with a 97% satisfaction rate. We start with a pre-training assessment to understand each participant's real needs, then build the course around measurable results — not generic content. We've delivered training for organizations including Chevron, OXY, and various central banks and government entities.
See what participants say in our client testimonials
Where does Gentex Training Center deliver its training courses?
Our training courses are delivered in major cities across the USA, Europe, Asia, and Africa, as well as through live interactive online sessions.
In which language do you offer your training courses?
Our primary language of instruction is English. We are also open to accommodating other languages depending on your training city and specific request.
How can I pay for the course?
You can pay via bank transfer to our USA or UAE accounts, credit card (American Express, Mastercard, or Visa).
Are certificates provided after completing a training course?
Yes. Upon successfully completing a training course, we issue our own certificate of completion.
