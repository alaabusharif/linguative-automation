## Design production stack

### Static
ChatGPT concept/copy → real approved assets and/or Higgsfield background/scene → Adobe Express Premium layout/typography/logo → QA → approval → Metricool

### Video
ChatGPT concept/script/storyboard → real footage/assets + Higgsfield where useful → Adobe finishing/captions/logo/resizing → QA → approval → Metricool/YouTube

The generative model is not trusted to render the final logo or important text.

## Expanded QA gate

A post cannot be marked approval-ready until all applicable checks pass:

1. Purpose — one clear business objective.
2. Three-second message — the viewer can understand the point quickly.
3. Logo — exact approved file, correct variant, present, undistorted, clear space respected.
4. Copy — spelling/grammar correct; no generic filler; claims supported.
5. Arabic — native phrasing, RTL, punctuation, line breaks and hierarchy work in Arabic.
6. Equipment — credible; real approved photo/reference used when identity matters; no invented controls/devices.
7. Layout — social-first, not slide-like; uncluttered; intentional hierarchy and negative space.
8. Text boxes — aligned; no overflow, clipping or inconsistent margins.
9. Overlays — full coverage; no seams/gaps.
10. Photo/video quality — crisp and relevant to the specific claim.
11. Contact/CTA — included when needed and legible.
12. Platform adaptation — correct aspect ratio/crop/caption/CTA; not lazy copy-paste.
13. Arabic/English ratio state — rolling ratio remains on target.
14. Static/video ratio state — rolling ratio remains on target.
15. Confidentiality — no unapproved identifying detail.
16. Export fidelity — inspect the actual final artifact, not only the source design.
17. No accidental variant — no historical/superseded logo or design asset.
18. Approval package — visual/video + caption + channel + proposed timing are ready.

## Retirement test for old design skill

The `linguative-design` skill becomes archival fallback after the new stack produces at least **6 consecutive accepted posts** covering at least 2 Arabic, 2 English, 2 static, 2 video/motion outputs where applicable, and one equipment-led creative, with no brand/logo/equipment failure.
