- Several first-touch outreach emails sent 2026-10-20 to embassy
  cultural/development offices got a polite "we'll keep you on file, will
  reach out if an opportunity arises" response — a soft, non-committal
  reply pattern typical of diplomatic missions; not a rejection, but not a
  lead either. Three instances this period, same pattern.
- One mid-size peacebuilding/training NGO replied positively to a first-touch
  outreach email (2026-10-14), specifying that transcription, subtitling,
  and video localization sit with a different internal department than the
  one first contacted — useful signal that outreach to multi-department
  NGOs should route a copy to the comms/digital-content function, not only
  procurement, for subtitling/localization-angle pitches.
- The combined-services pitch ("interpreters + owned equipment + AV +
  technical support" as one vendor) is the consistent thread across the
  deals that are actually moving (ASI LTA, KACE event, SPARK), versus the
  generic "translation & transcription support" subject lines used in the
  bulk Lead-Gen outreach batches, which are getting a lower response rate
  so far (mostly no reply, a few polite holding replies, one decline).
