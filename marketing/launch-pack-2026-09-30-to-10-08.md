## Simultaneous or consecutive? Choose by event design, not habit.

**Primary language:** English  
**Format:** Static 4:5  
**Objective:** Buyer education and better-qualified RFQs.  
**CTA:** Ask us which format fits your event.

### Artwork
Single premium comparison — not a slide deck.

**Left:** SIMULTANEOUS  
Conferences • Panels • Continuous flow • Larger audiences

**Right:** CONSECUTIVE  
Bilateral meetings • Site visits • Smaller discussions • Natural pauses

Small footer line: **The right choice depends on agenda, audience, timing and room format.**

### LinkedIn caption
“Do we need simultaneous or consecutive interpretation?” is not really a language question. It is an event-design question.

**Simultaneous interpretation** is usually the better fit when the program needs to keep moving continuously — conferences, panels, presentations and larger multilingual audiences.

**Consecutive interpretation** can work very well for smaller discussions, bilateral meetings, visits and formats where natural pauses are acceptable.

The right choice depends on the agenda, audience size, timing, room layout and how participants are expected to interact.

If you are planning a multilingual event, we can recommend the interpretation format before you build the rest of the setup around it.
