# Linguative — Organic Buyer Targeting and Revenue Diversification
Effective: 2026-11-08. Internal strategy implementation; never authorizes public posting, sending or scheduling.

## Organic targeting is not demographic ad targeting
Build buyer-relevant content, find public demand signals, distribute through professional relationships and permitted communities, and measure qualified responses. Do not claim an unpaid post reaches a selected industry/job role. LinkedIn Page follower targeting, where available and eligible, is not a cold-prospect substitute.

## Buyer map

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Legal firms and business legal departments | Partners, legal operations | Cross-border agreement, filings | Legal/document translation | LinkedIn buyer education, relevant website |
| Banks, auditors, insurers, consultancies | Finance, compliance, communications | Reports, multilingual disclosure | Financial/technical translation; media when applicable | LinkedIn editorial content |

| Hotels, venues, event organizers, DMCs | Events and technical operations | Booked conferences or production brief | AV rental and technical support without mandatory interpretation | Local referrals, LinkedIn |
| Corporates, associations, government bodies | Marketing, procurement, operations | Conference, assembly, launch | Event coordination, signage, AV, interpretation | Case-study style proof without unapproved clients |
| Individuals and local SMEs | End customer or administrator | Personal/business documentation | Available translation | Facebook, GBP, search |

Classify each candidate as DIRECT BUYER, INTERMEDIARY, or REFERRAL PARTNER. Use only public, permitted demand indicators; a signal is not proof of purchasing intent. Flag remote international language work separately from on-site physical delivery constraints. Prioritize opportunities by repeat demand, feasibility, margin when known, buying access and likelihood of qualifying, not audience size alone.

## Seven revenue pillars
Translation; transcription and other language-content services; interpretation; standalone conference technology/AV; events and production; photography/videography/video production; corporate/event visibility and branding.
Localization, proofreading, dubbing and subtitling may be described as offerings, but never imply a proven revenue track record that has not been verified. Do not advertise certified/accredited translation without documented authorization.

## Required fields for every master post brief
Service pillar | target segment | purchasing role | problem and real demand trigger | direct/intermediary/referral | geography and feasibility | service offer | evidence and client permissions | channel-specific organic distribution | language and format | one CTA | verified website destination | tracking plan | success KPI | approval state.

## Publishing & distribution
Three master concepts/week, Sun/Tue/Thu, adapted appropriately to channels. Maintain the existing rolling 10-master matrix of 7 EN/3 AR and 6 static/4 video unless revised by Ala. In rolling 14 concepts include all seven pillars, with no adjacent repeats and no pillar above three; at least half on services other than interpretation and conference technology. Calendar is a planning instrument, not publishing authorization.
Use original licensed/approved media first; correct locked logo assets only. Do not use private client documents, identify events/clients without register permission, fabricate hardware, automate social comments, scrape professional profiles, or send bulk DMs. Professional community contributions should be manual and relevant. Sales/BD owns outbound relationships and CRM operations; Marketing owns briefs, channel strategy and measurement definitions.

## Measurement
Use campaign concept ID, pillar, segment, buyer function, geography, buyer type, source and CTA; monitor qualified enquiry, quote, won/lost and repeat business where available. Link with UTMs only once configured and verified. Do not infer lead attribution from impressions. Weekly assessment: qualified leads. Quarterly: earned revenue by pillar, repeat customers and diversification.
