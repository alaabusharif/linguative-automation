WHAT THIS PACK IS
Built from the exact approved final-frame image supplied in the chat. The wordmark and tagline are not retyped and are not AI-regenerated.

WHY PNG + MATTES INSTEAD OF A NEW VECTOR LOGO
The approved final artwork supplied here is a rendered image. A newly traced vector would no longer be pixel-identical. For that reason, the exact visual elements are preserved as full-frame transparent PNGs, while a vector SVG is supplied only as a reveal/mask guide for the gold ribbon.

RECOMMENDED FUSION METHOD
1. Keep the Runway film as your background / MediaIn.
2. Bring 02_Wordmark_Exact into Fusion once. Use the supplied mattes to reveal parts of that exact image rather than rebuilding or retyping the wordmark.
3. GOLD RIBBON: use 07_Gold_Ribbon_Reveal_Guide.svg or Gold_Ribbon_Reveal_Matte as the mask. Animate a left-to-right wipe or Write-On-style reveal across the mask. Add only a restrained glow to the moving leading edge.
4. LETTER CONSTRUCTION: reveal Letter_01 through Letter_09 sequentially. Suggested: 3-4 frames overlap; Scale 0.94 -> 1.00; opacity 0 -> 1; very small vertical drift (8-12 px maximum). Use Spline easing.
5. METALLIC SWEEP: after the letters are present, animate a narrow soft light band across Wordmark_Matte for about 12-16 frames.
6. TAGLINE: reveal 03_Tagline_Exact with a center-out horizontal mask rather than a plain dissolve.
7. FINAL SNAP: once all motion has stopped, merge/cut to 06_Final_Frame_Exact_1920x1080 and hold it perfectly still to the end. Because every layer is registered to the same 1920x1080 frame, there should be no position jump.

SUGGESTED TIMING FOR A ~3.3 SECOND BUILD
0.00-0.65  gold ribbon draws in
0.35-1.75  wordmark pieces construct with overlapping reveals
1.55-2.20  metallic highlight travels across the completed wordmark
2.10-2.75  tagline reveals

IMPORTANT
Use 06_Final_Frame_Exact_1920x1080 for the last beat. That guarantees the approved frame is the actual endpoint, even if the construction masks are intentionally stylized.
