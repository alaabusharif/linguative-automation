# Brief: Q4 opener: one team for the whole multilingual event

- **Post date:** Sun 4 Oct 2026 (first post after the rebrand window; normal rotation resumes)
- **Channels:** LinkedIn, Facebook, GBP
- **Pillar:** P1 One vendor + P7 Q4 booking
- **Segment:** Local B2B (NGOs, UN agencies, embassies, EU-funded projects, corporates, event organizers)
- **Status:** Brief. Nothing publishes without Ala's approval.

## Objective
Generate inquiries for November-December events by making the bundle the reason to call. Organizers planning Q4 workshops usually juggle three or four suppliers (interpreters, equipment rental, AV company, video). Linguative replaces all of them with one contract, one technical rehearsal and one person accountable.

## Audience
Program officers, logistics/admin staff and procurement at NGO/UN country offices in Amman; embassy and EU project teams; corporate event leads. They care about the event not failing in front of their director or donor.

## Key message
"Your Q4 event, one team: interpreters, conference technology, sound, hybrid setup and a technician on site. One contact from planning to the last session."

## Proof points
- Owned **Bosch DICENTIS** conference system and interpreter booth; Linguative's own technician runs the setup (don't give counts).
- Named, approved work: **IMF** (ongoing: interpretation, translation, language distribution, video/photo), **Expertise France** (multi-assignment justice-sector program, 2021-22). Pick one name at most for the image; both are fine in the caption.
- Q4 timing: Nov-Dec dates fill early because year-end workshops cluster. Say this as advice, not as a scarcity claim.

## Call to action
"Planning an event for November or December? Send us the date, venue and languages, and we'll come back with a complete setup proposal." LinkedIn: DM or info@linguative.net. Facebook: Messenger or call. GBP button: **Call now**.
