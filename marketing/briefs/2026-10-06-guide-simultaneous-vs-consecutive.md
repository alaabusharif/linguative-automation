## Objective
Help buyers pick the right mode before they write the RFQ. This makes Linguative the reference they check, and it heads off RFQs that ask for the wrong service. Inquiry is the end goal; saves and shares are the leading signal.

## Audience
People writing a TOR or RFQ for a workshop, training, site visit or high-level meeting, often without interpretation experience.

## Key message
The mode depends on format, audience size and time. **Consecutive:** small meetings, site visits, interviews, trainings where time can stretch; no booth needed. **Simultaneous:** conferences, multi-speaker panels, larger audiences, tight agendas; needs a booth, interpreter consoles, language distribution and a technician. Linguative delivers both, and supplies the equipment for simultaneous.
