## Objective
Separate Linguative from translation offices, and from rivals who rent equipment in, by showing that the conference system is **owned and run by Linguative's own technician**. Owned kit means fewer handoffs, a rehearsed setup and one accountable team. This is the main differentiator against the competitors that sell the same bundle (per the roster, none of them publicly names an owned equipment brand).

## Audience
People who have had a microphone or channel fail mid-session and don't want it again. Event organizers and DMCs who subcontract the technical side.

## Key message
"We don't rent our conference system from someone else. We own it, we set it up, and our technician stays with it until the last session."

## Proof points
- Owned **Bosch DICENTIS** conference system and interpreter desks; interpreter booth; infrared language distribution; conference sound; wireless/delegate/lavalier microphones. **Name only Bosch DICENTIS.** No other brands, no counts or capacities.
- On-site technician for every simultaneous setup.
- Rehearsal/sound check before doors open (standard practice; copy can describe it as "we test every channel before your first speaker").

"Need a conference system with interpretation for a Q4 event? Ask for a setup proposal." LinkedIn: DM / info@linguative.net. Facebook: Messenger or call. GBP button: **Call now**.
