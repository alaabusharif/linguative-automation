# Brief: Hybrid meetings with interpretation: remote participants hear the interpreter too

- **Post date:** Sun 25 Oct 2026
- **Channels:** LinkedIn, Facebook, GBP
- **Pillar:** P6 Hybrid
- **Segment:** Local + regional B2B: UN/NGO regional offices, EU projects, embassies, corporates with regional teams
- **Status:** Brief. Nothing publishes without Ala's approval.

## Objective
Sell the hybrid add-on, which raises the value of a basic interpretation job (cameras, remote audio, interpretation routed to Zoom/Teams, recording, technician). Regional organizations that bring some participants to Amman and connect the rest online are the core buyers.

## Audience
Organizers of regional meetings where part of the audience is remote, and teams that have had a hybrid session where remote participants couldn't hear the interpretation.

## Key message
"In a hybrid meeting, remote participants should get the interpretation too, not just the room audio. We connect the interpreters, conference system, cameras and Zoom/Teams so everyone follows in their language, and a technician runs it all."
