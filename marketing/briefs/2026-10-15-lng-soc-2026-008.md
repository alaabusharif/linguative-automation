# LNG-SOC-2026-008 — Event continuity starts before event day

**Date:** 2026-10-15  
**Language:** English  
**Format:** Video / Reel / Short  
**Status:** Approved strategic direction; production brief. Final public asset remains QA/approval-gated.

## Business objective
Show that dependable multilingual event delivery comes from preparation, testing and technical verification — not from equipment alone.

## Core message
**When participation plans change, the multilingual experience should remain dependable.**

Linguative's value is the accountable delivery chain behind the room: interpretation planning, conference technology, AV/hybrid integration, rehearsals, signal checks and event-day technical verification.

## Content structure (20–45 sec)
1. Hook: room / booth / console preparing before participants arrive.
2. Test: interpreter audio and microphone path checked.
3. Route: language distribution / participant audio verified.
4. Connect: in-room and remote participation checked where applicable.
5. Listen: technician confirms the participant experience.
6. Close: "Test. Route. Connect. Verify." + CTA.
