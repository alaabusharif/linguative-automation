# LNG-SOC-2026-009 — Institutional buyer's checklist for multilingual conference delivery

**Date:** 2026-10-18  
**Language:** English  
**Format:** Static / carousel-ready  
**Status:** Approved strategic direction; production brief. Final public asset remains QA/approval-gated.

## Business objective
Help procurement and event teams evaluate a multilingual-event supplier on delivery readiness, not just unit prices or an equipment list.

## Core message
**A multilingual event should be scoped as one delivery system.**

## Checklist
Before appointing a supplier, confirm:
1. Interpretation format and language requirements are defined.
2. Interpreter working conditions and preparation are planned.
3. Conference technology and participant audio are matched to the room.
4. AV / hybrid requirements are integrated rather than handled in isolation.
5. Testing and technical support responsibilities are clearly assigned.
6. Remote participation is rehearsed when included.
7. One accountable point of contact owns the complete delivery scope.
8. Contingencies and event-day verification are included in the plan.
