## 1. October commercial objective

October is not a “posting month.” It is a **pipeline-building month**.

The goal is to make Linguative memorable for one clear idea:

> **One premium partner for the entire multilingual event — interpreters, conference interpretation technology, hybrid support, AV and multilingual content.**

October content must do one or more of the following:
1. generate qualified inquiries;
2. make Linguative easier to shortlist;
3. prove technical/event competence;
4. educate buyers before they request a quote;
5. create reusable authority assets for the website, proposals and sales conversations.

## 3. Operating rules

- **3 master concepts per week**, adapted by channel.
- Primary-language sequence repeats every 10 concepts: **7 EN / 3 AR**.
- Format sequence repeats every 10 concepts: **6 static / 4 video**.
- Do not translate the same caption mechanically across platforms.
- Arabic is written natively in polished professional MSA.
- Every visual uses one of the two locked logo assets only.
- Identifiable equipment must be real/approved or based on faithful reference; no invented conference technology.
- Client names appear only when the Client Reference Register permits the intended context.
- Every concept has one business objective and one CTA.
- Migration work runs in parallel; it does not delay this calendar.

## 6. Platform adaptation rules

### LinkedIn
- English-led B2B authority; Arabic concepts remain Arabic when strategically chosen.
- 80–160 word captions normally.
- Proof, planning insight, procurement relevance, technical credibility.
- CTA should be professional and low-friction.

### Instagram
- Static: 4:5, visually premium, minimal copy on artwork.
- Video: 9:16 Reels, strong first 1–2 seconds, captions/subtitles.
- Use real equipment/event footage whenever available.
- Caption can be shorter and more visual than LinkedIn.

### Facebook
- Adapt for local relevance and easier inquiry behavior.
- Arabic concepts remain strong authority pieces, not simplified filler.
- English concepts may remain English when aimed at institutional buyers; do not mechanically translate every post.

### Google Business Profile
- 80–150 words maximum where practical.
- Direct service/location relevance.
- Clear CTA: Call / Request a quote / Ask about equipment.
- Use real service/equipment imagery.
- No hashtags unless there is a specific reason.

### YouTube Shorts
- Use the four video concepts in each 10-post cycle as native Shorts.
- 20–45 seconds normally.
- Hook → demonstration/explanation → one CTA.
- Title and description should be searchable, not clickbait.
