## Flags for live Linguative pursuits

- **December Dead Sea conference (human rights / climate).** The event is publicly confirmed as the 15th International Conference of National Human Rights Institutions, 2-4 Dec 2026 in Jordan, on climate change and human rights ([GANHRI](https://ganhri.org/international-conference/)). Al-Oula has just run a two-day, government-backed festival with an exhibition at the same Dead Sea convention centre. That makes it the most credible bundled rival if this goes to competitive procurement. Expect it to pitch on venue experience and scale (65 simultaneous events, 40+ freelance interpreters, per its [site](http://www.al-oulaecs.com/about)).
- **Banking-sector work.** Al-Oula now has a public two-year track record with the Central Bank of Jordan and the Association of Banks ([Petra](https://petra.gov.jo/en/news/jordan-fintech-festival-opens-at-dead-sea-bringing-together-global-industry-leaders)), which is a useful reference for any bank's procurement committee.
- **NGO/donor/UN work.** COMCRA (GIZ, ETF, ILO, USAID/EU frameworks) and Range (now pitching international organizations, embassies and NGOs) are both in the same pool. Al-Oula works with GIZ and is on DevelopmentAid. Context: an ILO RFQ on UNGM in June 2026 asked for one "event management company" to cover hotel, conference facilities, interpretation and transport ([UNGM](https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/300950)). UN buyers do issue bundled RFQs directly, and bundled event firms are the natural bidders.
